# Audit: CONDITIONS slice (statuses, volatiles, side conditions, field, stat stages)

## Summary

Slice: CONDITIONS (statuses, volatiles, side and slot conditions, weather, terrain, pseudo-weather, stat stages). Rows audited: 108 (a row can cover several moves, e.g. V06 covers 7 partial-trap moves, V43 six no-op volatiles). Showdown conditions that can occur in `[Gen 9 Champions] BSS Reg M-C`: 6 major statuses, 11 reachable `conditions.ts` volatile conditions (confusion, flinch, trapped, trapper, partiallytrapped, lockedmove, twoturnmove, choicelock, mustrecharge, futuremove, stall), about 45 move/ability/item-embedded volatile or slot conditions, 8 side/slot conditions, 4 usable weathers + 3 primal, 4 terrains and 4 pseudo-weather rooms (Trick Room, Gravity, Magic Room, Wonder Room, Fairy Lock = 5), plus the stat-stage system: roughly 100 conditions. (Hail, Dynamax, Z-move heal replacement, Gems, Commander pairs are unreachable and excluded.)

Verdicts (108 rows): MATCH 29, PLAIN 1, LOGIC 37, PARTIAL 13, MISSING 27, DATA 1, CORE 0, UNSURE 0. K3, K4 and K5 sit inside three LOGIC rows (V01, V07, V06) because each also has non-K differences. By implementation level: fully implemented 30 rows (MATCH+PLAIN), partly implemented 51 rows (LOGIC+PARTIAL+DATA), not implemented at all 27 rows (MISSING).

Five most important findings:
1. SLEEP CLAUSE IS ACTIVE IN THE ENGINE but the format has none (GI:834-836). Any second sleep application on the opposing side is refused (S03).
2. Inert volatiles that real teams use: King's Shield (0.9% pool, also stuck forever after one use), Glaive Rush (1.5%, never applied), Throat Chop, Focus Energy, Unburden, Curse on Ghosts (1.5%), Smack Down, Disable, Torment, Ingrain, Aqua Ring, Octolock (V15, V33, V37, V27, V46, V24).
3. Six legal duration items are missing from the Items enum: Light Clay (screens 8 turns), Terrain Extender, Damp/Heat/Smooth/Icy Rock; plus Binding Band (SC05-07, F01-F04, F11, V06).
4. Screens: Brick Break and Psychic Fangs never break them, Defog wipes the USER's own screens, Safeguard never expires (SC05-SC09, SC15, SC18).
5. Freeze has no 3rd-attempt guaranteed thaw and no thaw from being hit by Fire moves or Scald/Scorching Sands (S04); Toxic damage rounds multiply-then-floor and is uncapped (S06); Encore/Taunt lengths differ from Showdown depending on who moves first (V19, V21).

Non-MATCH conditions ranked by how common they are in play (pool / ladder share from the coverage CSVs; statuses and weather first because every battle touches them):
1. S10 residual order, S09 check order, S06 toxic rounding, S03 sleep clause, S04 freeze (every game with status).
2. V19 Encore (7.8% pool), V36 Roost (6.7%), V12-V16 Protect family second-mover gap and King's Shield (5.5% / 0.9-1.3%), V07 Outrage (3.7%, K4), V17 Substitute (2.4%).
3. F11 Terrain Extender (1.3%), SC05-SC07 Light Clay (1.8-2.4%), SC18 Psychic Fangs (1.7%) and Brick Break (0.4%), V24 Curse (1.5%), V33 Glaive Rush (1.5%), V45 Baton Pass transfer (1.5%), V21 Taunt (1.0-1.3%), F04 Blizzard/Slush Rush/Ice Body in Snowscape (Blizzard 1.0-1.2%), V46 Unburden (1.0%), S07 Rest (1.2%), V18 Leech Seed (0.8%), V37 Throat Chop (0.2% pool / 1.6% ladder), V08 Dig/Fly/Dive exceptions (Earthquake is 16% of the pool).
4. F02 Damp Rock (0.6-1.2%), F03 Smooth Rock, SC13/SC15 Rapid Spin / Defog, SC04 Sticky Web, V27 Focus Energy, V11 Future Sight, T09 White Herb, V31-V52 minor volatiles, F14-F17 Gravity / Magic Room / Wonder Room / Fairy Lock (all under 0.2%).

Fix cost: about 35 rows are S size (a flag, counter or constant), about 40 M, and 5 L (Gravity, Magic Room, Wonder Room need new state fields).

## Method and build facts

- Reference: `pcsa\.tools\vendor\pokemon-showdown` (called SD below), mod `champions`. Engine: `research\pcsa-rl\poke-engine-eval\poke-engine\src` (called ENG), `genx` engine. File short names: GI = genx\generate_instructions.rs, GS = genx\state.rs, CE = genx\choice_effects.rs, DC = genx\damage_calc.rs, AB = genx\abilities.rs, IT = genx\items.rs, ST = state.rs, CH = choices.rs. SD files: cond = data\conditions.ts, mv = data\moves.ts, champ-cond = data\mods\champions\conditions.ts, champ-mv = data\mods\champions\moves.ts.
- Cargo features `champions` and `bss` select `genx` with NO genN feature (lib.rs:143-153 asserts `champions` is exclusive with gen2..gen9). So all `cfg(feature="gen9")` blocks are OFF, `cfg(any(...,"champions"))` blocks are ON, and `terastallization` (needs gen9) is OFF.
- ENG state has NO field for Gravity, Magic Room, Wonder Room, Fairy Lock, Mud/Water Sport, Ion Deluge (ST:1321 State = side_one, side_two, weather, terrain, trick_room, 3 flags).
- ENG volatile durations exist only for confusion, encore, lockedmove, slowstart, taunt, yawn (ST:787). Every other volatile is a bare on/off bit with no timer. A bit that is set but whose enum name is never read in genx code is called "inert" below (verified by grepping `PokemonVolatileStatus::NAME` across src, tests excluded).
- Legality: ids not present in `03-coverage-moves.csv` / `-abilities.csv` / `-items.csv` were treated as NOT legal in this format and were not audited (Heavy-Duty Boots, Safety Goggles, Mirror Herb, Clear Amulet, Covert Cloak, Choice Band/Specs, Toxic/Flame Orb, Black Sludge, Booster Energy, Lucky Chant, Mist, Crafty Shield, Mat Block, Silk Trap, Burning Bulwark, Obstruct, Heal Block, Embargo, Telekinesis, Nightmare, Grudge, Laser Focus, Tar Shot, Powder, Rage, Bide, Defense Curl, Charge, Mud Sport, Water Sport, Ion Deluge, Doom Desire, Lunar Dance, Hail, Magma Storm/Clamp/Thunder Cage). Primal weather abilities, Cloud Nine, Air Lock, Light Clay, Terrain Extender, Damp/Heat/Smooth/Icy Rock, Binding Band, Safeguard, Gravity, Magic Room, Wonder Room, Fairy Lock, Quick Guard, Wide Guard ARE legal.
- Column `used` is the CSV `used` flag of the owning move/ability/item (1 = in our data), 1 for core statuses and weather.

## Notes (items that do not fit a row)

1. Weather rocks, Light Clay and Terrain Extender are six distinct legal items that fall into `UNKNOWNITEM` (CSV status d). Adding them is one enum entry plus a duration read each; together they are 4-5% of item usage (Light Clay 1.8%, Terrain Extender 1.3%, Damp Rock 0.6%, Smooth Rock 0.3%, Heat Rock 0.1%).
2. Inert bits (set by a move, never read): AQUARING, ATTRACT, CURSE, DISABLE, ELECTRIFY, FOCUSENERGY, GASTROACID, IMPRISON, INGRAIN, KINGSSHIELD, MINIMIZE, OCTOLOCK, POWERTRICK, SMACKDOWN, STOCKPILE, SYRUPBOMB, THROATCHOP, TORMENT, UNBURDEN (read but never set), GLAIVERUSH (read but never set), and the side counters QuickGuard, WideGuard, MatBlock, CraftyShield, LuckyChant, Mist, Safeguard (the last two never expire, GI:2951-3001).
3. Ability bugs seen while reading condition code, for the ability slice: Speed Boost (AB:1319-1328) boosts at the end of the entry turn (SD skips the turn the mon came in, `activeTurns`), Solar Power (AB:1271-1286) caps its damage at MISSING HP (`maxhp - hp`) instead of current HP, Shed Skin always cures instead of 1/3 (AB:1380-1382), Ice Body and Slush Rush ignore Snowscape (F04), Sand Veil / Snow Cloak have no effect.
4. Sleep Clause (S03) is the single most dangerous silent rule difference for the learning agent: it makes the engine think a second sleep move is useless, so an RL policy trained on it would never learn double-sleep lines that the real format allows.
5. `moves_first` switch-vs-switch ignores Trick Room and deterministic ties go to side two (K-level); weather suppression by Cloud Nine/Air Lock is already right.
6. Not traced: Primal weather replacement rules (F05), Healing Wish when no replacement exists (SC11), Stockpile stat side (V40), Beak Blast (V42) and Choice-lock re-enable after item loss (V09) were judged from grep and one read each; confidence is marked low/medium on those rows.
7. The residual-order table in S10 assumes `battle.ts:517-527` ordering (prior report row 23); I did not re-derive per-handler sub-orders inside order 26-28.
