## Notes

M1 (shared by all 36 Mega stone rows, any stone). The stone is protected from Knock Off, Thief and Trick/Switcheroo when it is on the target: `item_is_permanent` (ENG state.rs:617-708) covers all stones, Knock Off checks it (choice_effects.rs:518, :843) and Trick checks `defender.item_can_be_removed()` (choice_effects.rs:1587). But the Trick/Switcheroo arm never checks the user's own item. A Mega stone holder that uses Trick or Switcheroo hands the stone away. Showdown (moves.ts:18640-18667, trick same) fails the move because `source.takeItem()` returns false for the holder's stone. Fix: add `!attacker.item_can_be_removed()` to the check at choice_effects.rs:1587. Size S. Also check Bestow-like moves in another slice.

N1. Mega Evolution mapping is correct for every stone in the slice (species + stone to the right Mega id, types, ability and base stats compared against pokedex.ts), except the five DATA rows above. One Mega per side is enforced (state.rs:1097 `can_use_mega`). K8 (Mega-turn speed) applies to all stones: GI:3891-3903 orders Mega evolutions by pre-Mega speed. The first match check in `mega_evolve_target` also requires `item` to equal the stone at the moment of evolving, which is fine.

N2. Item effects are applied inside `item_before_move` (GI:1687) before accuracy, Protect, Substitute and immunity are resolved. This affects every one-shot item (gems, damage-reduction berries): they are consumed whether or not the move reaches the target. Fix in one place by moving consumption to after the hit is confirmed.

N3. Choice items (belong to another slice but touched here): lock is applied by disabling other moves at move use, including when the move later fails; Showdown also drops the lock if the Choice item is lost (Trick, Knock Off). Low priority.

N4. Items with a wrong or odd value outside this slice noticed in items.rs: Weakness Policy gives +2 Atk and +2 SpA (correct), but Eviolite and Assault Vest halve BP on incoming moves rather than boosting the defender's stat; these are K1-like rounding only. Champions marks many items Past (not legal): choicespecs, choiceband, weaknesspolicy, assaultvest, flameorb, toxicorb, blacksludge, eviolite, loadeddice, throatspray, booster energy and others; engine behaviour for those is irrelevant to Reg M-C.

N5. Engine does not model Heal Block against Leftovers or Sitrus (Showdown blocks both). Low priority.

N6. `generate_instructions` for Lum Berry (GI:886) cures by refusing to set the status. If the same Lum holder is poisoned by Toxic Spikes or hit by Static, the status is set and cured at its next move or end of turn (Showdown cures at once).
