# Abilities audit 01 (CSV lines 2-54)

## Summary

Slice: ABILITIES, CSV lines 2-54 (53 rows). Rows audited: 53 of 53. NOT AUDITED: none.

Verdict counts: MATCH 18, PLAIN 0, DATA 2, LOGIC 24, PARTIAL 2, MISSING 6, CORE 1, UNSURE 0.

Five most important findings:
1. prankster: every Status move (Protect, Recover, Swords Dance, Tailwind, Reflect, weather, hazards) is nullified when the opponent is Dark-type; SD only fails foe-targeted ones (E-ab:1879).
2. disguise: busts before accuracy/sleep/flinch/Protect/type-immunity checks, zeroes secondaries, zeroes all hits of multi-hit moves (E-ab:590, called from E-gi:2255).
3. protean/libero: dual-type holders are not retyped when one type matches; fires even when the user is asleep/flinched/paralysed; TYPECHANGE volatile shared with Soak.
4. Missing abilities with real pool share: emergencyexit, cursedbody, auraguard (not even in the enum), stancechange, steadfast, unburden (volatile never applied).
5. magicbounce does not reflect stat-lowering moves (boost target not flipped); speedboost activates on the switch-in turn; mold breaker is not consulted for Sturdy, status/stat-drop/flinch immunities, Contrary, Battle/Shell Armor.

Others worth knowing: thermalexchange and justified trigger on status moves (free +1 Atk from Will-O-Wisp / Taunt); roughskin ignores Magic Guard; iron fist misses Double Shock (punch flag); sharpness misses Crush Claw and Dire Claw (slicing flag); contact-ability and Weak Armor effects are rolled once per move, not per hit.

- N2: Damage-modifying abilities are folded into `base_power` as floats (x1.5, x1.2, /2 ...) while SD applies 4096-based chainModify on Atk/SpA, base power or final damage with rounding at each step. Same family as K1; not itemised per row.
- N3: Atk-stat abilities (Huge Power, Pure Power, Gorilla Tactics, Hustle, Guts, Defeatist, Toxic/Flare Boost style) are implemented on base_power for any Physical move, so they also apply to Body Press (SD modifies Def) and Foul Play (SD uses the target's Atk and the target's modifiers). Edge cases only.
- N4: Secondary effects are rolled once per move after the hit loop (E-gi:3660 comment). Every defender ability implemented as an attacker-side secondary (Static, Flame Body, Weak Armor, Thermal Exchange, Justified, Steam Engine, Water Compaction, Gooey, Tangling Hair, Poison Point, Effect Spore ...) therefore differs from SD on multi-hit moves, and those that use `target: User` with a boost effect (Gooey, Tangling Hair) also fire through a Substitute (E-gi:1072 only skips Opponent-target secondaries; statuses are skipped via hit_sub). Gooey/Tangling Hair are in another slice.
- N5: Secondaries on Status moves are not suppressed, so any ability implemented as a type-based secondary (E-ab:2412-2445, 2801-2816, 2785-2800, 2911-2929) fires on Status moves of that type. Seen in thermalexchange and justified; also affects Steam Engine, Water Compaction, Rattled (other slices).
- N6: Evasion boosts are never used in hit chance (E-gi:1230 uses only accuracy_boost). Relevant to Unaware, Keen Eye, Sand Veil, Snow Cloak, Double Team/Minimize if legal.
- N7: Intimidate, Clear Body etc. share apply_boost_instruction (E-gi:928). Its guard `target_side_ref != attacking_side_ref && immune_to_stats_lowered_by_opponent` is applied to positive boosts too, and has no attacker-ability input, which is the root of the Mold Breaker gap in clearbody/contrary/moldbreaker.

- N9: Is_grounded (E-st:720) ignores Ingrain, Smack Down, Gravity and Magnet Rise-style states except via separate paths; affects Levitate, terrains, hazards. Belongs to a moves/field slice.
- N10: Heal Pulse has no heal in the move table (choices.rs:7374); Grassy Terrain halves only Earthquake (E-dc:239), not Bulldoze/Magnitude and without a grounded-target check. Reported for the moves/field slices.
