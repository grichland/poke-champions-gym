## Five most important findings

1. **Slush Rush never fires in Snow** (gi.rs:2549 tests `Weather::HAIL` only; Showdown `hail` or `snowscape`). Snow Warning in the engine sets SNOW under `champions`, so the doubling is dead in practice. Also reads raw `weather_type`, so Air Lock / Cloud Nine do not stop it.
2. **Air Lock / Cloud Nine suppression is inconsistent**: honoured by `weather_is_active` and damage_calc, ignored by the Chlorophyll/Swift Swim/Sand Rush/Slush Rush speed doubling (gi.rs:2541), Weather Ball (ce.rs:460), Morning Sun/Moonlight/Synthesis (ce.rs:301) and the Solar Beam/Blade half-power test (ce.rs:485).
3. **Bad Dreams (Mega Darkrai) ignores Magic Guard and Comatose foes; Aftermath ignores Magic Guard and Damp.** In Showdown both are ability-sourced damage, which Magic Guard blocks. Mega Darkrai Bad Dreams is a live Champions case.
4. **Liquid Ooze is half done**: negated drain is not clamped to the drainer's HP (can push HP negative), and Leech Seed (gi.rs:3217-3253) heals the seeder even when the seeded Pokemon has Liquid Ooze (Showdown damages the seeder).
5. **Whole abilities with no effect**: Sand Veil (no 0.8 accuracy, no sand immunity), Big Pecks, Anger Point, Anger Shell, Cheek Pouch, Dancer, Forecast, Flower Gift. Plus wrong ones: Battery boosts the user's own special moves (Showdown: ally only, nothing in singles), Flare Boost boosts physical moves (Showdown: Special only), Wandering Spirit turns the attacker into Mummy instead of swapping abilities, Dark Aura / Aura Break are one-sided (foe's Dark moves not boosted, Aura Break never reduces to 0.75), Dauntless Shield re-triggers every switch-in (Showdown: once per battle), Beast Boost picks the highest boosted stat (Showdown: unboosted).

## Notes

- Row `liquidooze` difference text: the Magic Guard remark is only a side thought; Showdown Liquid Ooze damage has no Magic Guard exemption of its own (effect is an ability, so Magic Guard WOULD block `this.damage(damage)` only when the source is a non-Move effect; check not exercised). Treat only items (a) and (b) as confirmed.
- `wanderingspirit` difference, plainly: Showdown swaps the two abilities (attacker gets Wandering Spirit, holder gets the attacker's ability, both re-run onStart). Engine only overwrites the attacker's ability with Mummy and leaves the holder unchanged. Engine also does not check failskillswap abilities.
- Absorb-style abilities (Volt Absorb, Water Absorb, Storm Drain, Lightning Rod, Sap Sipper, Motor Drive and others) are implemented by rewriting the move, and the default move target is Opponent (choices.rs:20672). Any field or hazard-like move of the right type that has no explicit target (Electric Terrain, Ion Deluge, Rain Dance, Water Sport, Grassy Terrain, and similar) is probably captured. Not audited here beyond Volt Absorb; worth a sweep in the Moves/Abilities slices.
- Moves blocked by abilities are modelled as accuracy 0 (Dazzling, Soundproof, Bulletproof, Queenly Majesty, Overcoat, Damp). A zero-accuracy outcome goes through the miss path (gi.rs:1235-1260): Blunder Policy and crash damage would trigger, which Showdown does not do for ability blocks.
- Engine bug in another slice: Blizzard accuracy bypass tests HAIL only (ce.rs:492), so it never fires in Snow. Ice Body (abil:1288) and Hail damage (gi.rs:3005) are also HAIL-only; Champions has Snow only.
- Engine bug in another slice: Sand weather damage list (gi.rs:3029) lacks Sand Force / Sand Rush immunity.
- Engine bug in another slice: Solar Power (abil:2075) and Leaf Guard (gi.rs:791) ignore Desolate Land.
- Engine bug in another slice: `strong_winds_block_weather` (st:289) only covers Delta Stream; Primordial Sea and Desolate Land should also stop Drought, Drizzle, Sand Stream, Snow Warning and the weather moves.
- Mega abilities in this slice that matter for Champions: Mega Darkrai Bad Dreams, Mega Zeraora Volt Absorb, Mega Zygarde Aura Break, Mega Tatsugiri Commander (no-op in singles).
- Carrier legality noted from champions formats-data: Cherrim, Kecleon, Archeops, Charjabug, Vikavolt are `Past` (illegal); Castform, Greninja, Emolga, Dedenne are legal. Legality was not checked for every carrier of every ability above.
- Timing note: engine runs ability/item end-of-turn effects (gi.rs:3205-3214) after status damage; Showdown runs weather-linked heals (Rain Dish, Ice Body) first and abilities such as Bad Dreams later (order 28). Only Rain Dish ordering is flagged above.
