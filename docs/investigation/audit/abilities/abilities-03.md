Five most important findings:
1. **Sheer Force** (`abilities.rs:2233-2249`) deletes `volatile_status` on every move that has one, so Status moves lose their effect (Protect, Taunt, Encore, Leech Seed, Confuse Ray, Focus Energy; `choices.rs:12694`, `17154`, `4762`, `9501`, `2776`, `6039`). Showdown only strips `secondaries` and `self` of moves that have secondaries.
2. **Guts** (`abilities.rs:2293`) and **Solar Power** (`:2074`) multiply base power with no category check, so special moves get the Guts boost and physical moves get the Solar Power boost. Showdown: Guts is Atk only, Solar Power is SpA only. Solar Power's end-of-turn damage (`:1271-1287`) is `min(maxhp/8, maxhp-hp)`, a copy of the heal formula, so it deals no damage at full HP and less than 1/8 when slightly hurt.
3. **Mold Breaker is never honoured for status, stat-drop and volatile immunities** (`immune_to_status` `generate_instructions.rs:776-863`, `immune_to_stats_lowered_by_opponent` `state.rs:782`, `volatile_status_can_be_applied` `state.rs:777`). Insomnia, Magma Armor, Purifying Salt, Sweet Veil, Hyper Cutter, Aroma Veil are all `breakable` in Showdown. The same holds for the Mold Breaker, Teravolt, Turboblaze and Mycelium Might arms.
4. **Missing abilities**: Snow Cloak (x0.8 accuracy in snow), Tangled Feet (x0.5 accuracy vs confused), Mimicry, Cud Chew, Rivalry (the engine has no gender field), Flower Veil. All are in the mold-breaker list or the enum only.
5. **Probability and ordering errors**: Effect Spore uses three independent rolls 9/10/11 (real: one roll, 11 sleep / 10 par / 9 psn, so engine totals 27.1% vs 30%, sleep 9.0% vs 11%), and ignores powder immunity (Grass, Overcoat, Safety Goggles). Shed Skin always cures (real 33%). Hydration and Shed Skin cure after burn/poison damage (Showdown cures before). Sand Rush and Sand Force still take sandstorm damage (Showdown: immune). Dry Skin never takes sun damage.

## Notes

- Cross-cutting: `immune_to_status`, `immune_to_stats_lowered_by_opponent` and `volatile_status_can_be_applied` never call `ignores_target_ability`. One fix (pass the attacker and move into these three checks) closes 6 rows (insomnia, magmaarmor, purifyingsalt, sweetveil, hypercutter, aromaveil) and the rest of the breakable status/stat immunities (Limber, Immunity, Vital Spirit, Water Veil, Oblivious/Own Tempo volatile checks, etc.).
- Cross-cutting: User-target secondaries (contact abilities such as Effect Spore, Flame Body, Static, Poison Point, Gooey, Tangling Hair, Spicy Spray) still roll when the attack hits a Substitute (`generate_instructions.rs:1072` only skips Opponent-target secondaries), and are all skipped by the DEFENDER's Covert Cloak (`:3794`). Showdown triggers neither case.
- Cross-cutting (approximation, not a defect of one ability): attack-stat abilities are modelled as base-power multipliers by move category. They are wrong for Body Press, Foul Play and Psyshock-family moves, which use a different stat than their category suggests.
- Weather abilities set `weather_type` directly and `strong_winds_block_weather` only knows Delta Stream; Desolate Land and Primordial Sea also block ordinary weather in Showdown but are not in the Champions pool.
- Observed in another slice: `item_before_move` (items.rs:760) uses 1/4 for Sitrus Berry while `item_end_of_turn` (items.rs:915) uses 1/2; Showdown uses 1/2 always.
- Observed in another slice: end-of-turn weather damage (generate_instructions.rs:3004-3049) ignores Sand Veil, Sand Force, Sand Rush, Snow Cloak immunities; hail branch also gives Snow Cloak no immunity.
- `berserk` (CSV line 107) is outside this slice; reading `abilities.rs:956` showed its threshold test uses integer `maxhp/2` with `hp < max/2 && hp + dmg >= max/2` while Showdown uses `hp <= max/2 && hp + dmg > max/2`, an off-by-one at exactly 50%; the owner of that row should confirm.
