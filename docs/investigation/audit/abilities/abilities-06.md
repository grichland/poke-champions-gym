## Five most important findings
1. **Unseen Fist** (A:2256): the engine multiplies base power by 0.25 on every contact move, protected or not. Champions only applies x0.25 when the move actually bypasses Protect (champions/scripts.ts:298). The Unseen Fist user does a quarter of normal damage to unprotected targets.
2. **Truant** (choice_effects.rs:706): the Truant volatile is only set inside `choice_after_damage_hit`, so only after a damaging move that hit. Status moves, misses, Protect-blocked moves and flinched turns never start the loaf turn. Showdown sets it in onBeforeMove on every attempt.
3. **Toxic Boost** (A:2307) boosts every move 1.5x. Showdown boosts Physical moves only.
4. **Ability-injected secondaries fire on status moves**: Stench, Toxic Chain, Steam Engine, Water Compaction and Rattled add a secondary to a Status-category move too. A Stench user's Swords Dance can flinch the opponent. Will-O-Wisp on a Steam Engine target gives it +6 Speed. Showdown only reacts to damaging hits (onDamagingHit and onModifyMove with `category !== Status`).
5. **Sign-blind boost immunity** (GI:944-947, ST:782): `immune_to_stats_lowered_by_opponent` blocks positive boosts from the opponent as well. This affects White Smoke and Clear Body versus Swagger/Flatter, and any holder behind a Substitute. Absorb abilities (Storm Drain, Wind Rider, Well-Baked Body) therefore fail to boost when the holder has a Substitute, and Clear Amulet blocks them. Mold Breaker / Teravolt / Turboblaze are also not consulted for status immunity, stat-drop immunity or Sticky Hold.

Other notable gaps. Missing outright: Stall, Run Away (Champions override), Wimp Out, Soul-Heart, Simple (gen4 only in this build), Supersweet Syrup, Wind Power, Ripen, Zen Mode, Tera Shell, Tera Shift, Teraform Zero. Rattled lacks the Intimidate trigger. Wind Rider lacks the switch-in and Tailwind triggers. Contact reactions with User-target secondaries (Tangling Hair) still fire when the hit lands on a Substitute.

## Notes

- Legality caveat: champions `formats-data.ts` marks Slaking (Truant), Regigigas (Slow Start), Darmanitan (Zen Mode), Wishiwashi (Schooling), Minior (Shields Down), Magearna (Soul-Heart), Terapagos (Tera abilities), Rapidash and Dodrio (Run Away holders) as `isNonstandard: "Past"`. Holders of other abilities in this slice may be legal; I did not enumerate holders. Hydrapple (Supersweet Syrup) is legal (tier RU).
- Systemic: `apply_boost_instruction` (GI:944-947) calls `immune_to_stats_lowered_by_opponent` for any non-zero boost aimed at the other side, regardless of sign, and the function also returns true for a Substitute. Affects every absorb ability that boosts the holder (Storm Drain, Wind Rider, Well-Baked Body, Lightning Rod, Motor Drive, Sap Sipper), Swagger/Flatter against Clear Body / White Smoke / Clear Amulet, and holders with a Substitute. Belongs to the stat-boost slice.
- Systemic: reaction boosts aimed at the attacker (Tangling Hair, Gooey, Silk Trap) are passed as self-targeted boosts, so the attacker's Clear Body / White Smoke / Clear Amulet never blocks them (Showdown blocks, since the source is the holder).
- Systemic: `get_instructions_from_secondaries` skips only Opponent-target secondaries when a Substitute is hit (GI:1072). User-target reactions (Static, Flame Body, Poison Point, Effect Spore, Gooey, Tangling Hair) fire through a Substitute; Showdown does not.
- Systemic: Covert Cloak on the defender (GI:3794) discards all secondaries, including ability-injected reaction effects (Steam Engine, Rattled, Water Compaction, Tangling Hair) that Showdown does not block.
- Systemic: secondaries injected by ability code run for Status-category moves because `ability_modify_attack_against` / `_being_used` do not check category; fix per ability or filter once at injection.
- `Choice.accuracy` cannot represent "never misses" (default 100.0). Affects Wonder Skin here and any accuracy-modifying ability or item.
- Mold Breaker / Teravolt / Turboblaze bypass is implemented only through `ignores_target_ability` inside the attack-modifier functions (A:2332); it is absent from `immune_to_status`, `immune_to_stats_lowered_by_opponent` and `item_can_be_removed`.
- Showdown Truant (SD:5184) also has an onStart that adds the volatile on re-entry; not modelled.
- Flare Boost (A:2031, another slice) has the same missing category check as Toxic Boost (Special only in Showdown).
